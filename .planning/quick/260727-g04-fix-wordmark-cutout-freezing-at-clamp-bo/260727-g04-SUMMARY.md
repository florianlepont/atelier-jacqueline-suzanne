# Quick Task 260727-g04: Fix wordmark cutout freezing at clamp boundary during edge-click commit Summary

**`commitEdge()`'s full-slide commit now reverts the hero wordmark to its solid-ink fallback for the entire slide (mirroring `render()`'s existing pattern on every other nav path), instead of letting the quick-260727-drq clamp hold a frozen, wrongly-cropped slice of the departing photo pinned at its pixel boundary for the whole ~420-480ms commit.**

## Accomplishments
- `commitEdge()`'s full-slide branch now removes `has-wordmark-photo` and sets `--wordmark-photo: none` on `root` at the very start of the slide, so the wordmark shows solid ink for the slide's duration rather than a frozen photo-cutout slice.
- Removed the now-pointless `keepWordmarkSynced(600)` rAF sync pump from `commitEdge()` — the cutout is hidden for the whole slide, so there's nothing visible to keep synced; the existing `render()`-driven sync paths (`resetPeek()` -> `keepWordmarkSynced()`, `revealWordmarkPhoto()` -> `syncWordmarkAlignment()`) already re-establish alignment once the swap lands.
- Added a new FR+EN Playwright regression describe block (`carousel wordmark does not freeze during edge-click commit (quick-260727-g04)`) proving: (1) the wordmark is solid (no `has-wordmark-photo`) at a deterministic 150ms mid-slide sample, and (2) it re-reveals with a valid, freshly-computed `--wordmark-bg-position` once the commit settles on the new gallery.
