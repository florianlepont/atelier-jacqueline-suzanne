## Decisions Made
- **D-01 honored, mechanism unchanged:** kept the dim as plain CSS `opacity` on the same selector rather than switching to a scrim, overlay, `color-mix()`, or any other mechanism -- only the numeric value moved, per the plan's locked decision preserving the Romane-approved sketch-010 "B2 — Cursor Preview" pattern.
- **0.75, not the bare 0.717 floor:** chosen as the nearest clean quarter-step above the worst-case alpha floor, giving ~5% headroom (3.16:1 vs the 3:1 threshold) to absorb 8-bit compositor rounding and axe's own blend arithmetic, while staying as close to the original dim intensity as the constraint allows.
- **3:1 threshold, not 4.5:1:** the title is WCAG large text (display font, `clamp(32px, 5vw, 64px)`, semibold, desktop-only where `5vw >= 40px`); 4.5:1 is mathematically unreachable here (would require alpha ~0.99, i.e. no dimming at all).
