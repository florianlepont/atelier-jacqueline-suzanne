Privacy Policy

This page describes, simply and directly, the only data handled by this site: contact-form data, the site's own content, the host's technical logs, and a single functional cookie. This is not a formal legal document — it is a plain-language explanation of what actually happens.

Contact form

When you send a message via the contact form, it is sent directly to Romane Lepont's own mailbox, hosted with OVH, with no third-party processor involved.

Data controller: Romane Lepont / Atelier Jacqueline Suzanne, email: contact@atelierjacquelinesuzanne.fr.

Purpose: replying to your enquiry.

Legal basis: your consent — submitting the form is a voluntary action on your part.

Required fields: name, email, and message are required so we can reply to you. An additional hidden field (a "honeypot") is used only to detect spam robots — it never collects any data about you.

Recipient: Romane Lepont only — no third-party processor is involved.

Retention: your message is kept only as long as needed to reply to you, then deleted.

Your rights: you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and object to the processing of your data. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority).

Site content (Sanity)

The site's content (galleries, text) comes from a content management system (Sanity), queried only when the site is built. No data about you is ever sent to Sanity.

Hosting logs

Like any web host, OVH automatically logs your IP address and browser (user-agent) as a normal part of serving the site's pages. This counts as personal data under GDPR, even though this site's own code never touches it.

Cookies

This site uses a single cookie, named ajs_locale. It is only set when you explicitly click the FR/EN language switcher — never automatically on page load — and it stores only your language preference (French or English). It is set with the SameSite=Lax and Secure attributes, for a duration of about one year.

This cookie is strictly functional: it is only used to personalize the site's display in the language you choose. As such, it is exempt from consent under CNIL guidance — which is why this site shows no accept/reject cookie banner. This site uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies of any kind.
