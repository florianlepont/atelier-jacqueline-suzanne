PaysageStatement coming soon — artist's introduction to this series.
BrumeFog erases edges and slows time. Crosses, trees, stone — everything hangs suspended, uncertain. A passage between presence and disappearance.
SilosAcross silent plains, these giants of concrete and steel stand watch, alone. Still witnesses to a fading agricultural age, they trace an unintentional architecture — raw, patient, almost sacred.
The Victorian Tea roomMy work explores the intersection of cinematic narrative and the staging of intimacy. Through a meticulous arrangement of spaces, characters, and temporalities, I seek to capture the subtle tension between reality and fiction. Each project is an invitation to look behind the curtain, questioning how we construct memories and stories through the lens.
AdultsAdults is a black-and-white photographic series exploring nighttime scenes captured through car windows. Between intimacy and distance, the images reflect on suspended moments of adult life.
