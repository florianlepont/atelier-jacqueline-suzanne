The Victorian Tea room

The Victorian Tea room

My work explores the intersection of cinematic narrative and the staging of intimacy. Through a meticulous arrangement of spaces, characters, and temporalities, I seek to capture the subtle tension between reality and fiction. Each project is an invitation to look behind the curtain, questioning how we construct memories and stories through the lens.
