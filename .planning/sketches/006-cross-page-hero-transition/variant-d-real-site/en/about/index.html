Atelier Jacqueline Suzanne

About

Romane Lepont is a photographer who develops her artistic practice under the name Atelier Jacqueline Suzanne. Through a range of photographic series, she creates a sensitive visual world shaped by close attention to detail, texture, and fleeting moments. Her work explores photography’s ability to reveal what might otherwise remain unseen or go unnoticed in everyday life.

01

Studio & practice

The practice of Atelier Jacqueline Suzanne is centred on photography and developed through series conceived as complete bodies of work. From taking photographs to selecting the final images, each project is guided by close attention to light, texture, and the rhythm of visual storytelling. The work takes shape through photographic prints, editions, and a variety of artistic projects.

02

Medium & technique

Photography is the studio’s primary medium. Each image is shaped by close attention to composition, light, colour, and texture. The photographs are then carefully selected and prepared to preserve the visual coherence of each series and to support their presentation as prints or editions.
