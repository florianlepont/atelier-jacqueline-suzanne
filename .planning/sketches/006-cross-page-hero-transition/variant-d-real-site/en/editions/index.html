Rebut

Rebut is the printed edition that extends the photographic series of the same name. Produced in a limited run, it gathers in black and white what might have been thrown away and was chosen to keep instead — a handmade object, meant to circulate first among friends before finding its place elsewhere.

Silos

Silo print is a printed publication designed at the intersection of zines and cartographic design. Crafted to fit effortlessly into everyday life — even right into your pocket —, it unfolds into a bold visual experience blending dynamic typography, urban motifs, and visual documentation.
