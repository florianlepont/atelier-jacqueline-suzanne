    - `t = 0` gives `scale 1`, `wordmarkOpacity 1`, `photoOpacity 0`.
    - `t = 1` gives `scale 8.5`, `wordmarkOpacity 0`, `photoOpacity 1`.
    - `t = 0.5` gives `scale` close to `1.9375` (ease-in-cubic: `1 + 7.5 * 0.125`), `wordmarkOpacity 1`, `photoOpacity 0`.
    - `t = 0.9` gives `photoOpacity` close to `0.3333` and `wordmarkOpacity 1` (fade has not started below 0.92).
    - `t = 0.96` gives `wordmarkOpacity` close to `0.5`.
    - `t = -2` behaves as `t = 0`; `t = 4` behaves as `t = 1` (input is clamped, not trusted).
    - `scale` is strictly non-decreasing across an ascending sweep of `t` values.
