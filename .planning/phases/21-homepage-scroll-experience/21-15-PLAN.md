<objective>
Close the second half of `21-UAT.md` round-2 gap 2 (`.planning/debug/homepage-scroll-text-reveal-too-fast.md`): the GALLERY SLIDE descriptions, which still hang off `applyArrival()`'s symmetric 0.98 visibility threshold. Replace that threshold with a two-level latch (reveal high, release low), so text that has been revealed stays revealed until its slide is genuinely leaving — and carry the consolidated real-device human-check for all five round-2 gaps.

Purpose: the debug session names one shared root cause with two structurally distinct triggers. Plan 21-13 removed the first trigger entirely by rebuilding the intro as a pinned scrub with a trailing dwell, and took the intro out of the arrival pass. The second trigger is untouched and is the one this plan owns: `applyArrival()` toggles `is-revealed` on `ratio >= 0.98` with the SAME number used to show and to hide, evaluated fresh on every painted frame. There is no hysteresis and no dwell, so a single frame below 0.98 re-hides text the 180ms transition had only just finished bringing in. On a real phone that dip is not hypothetical: the deck's snap is `y proximity` (not `mandatory`, deliberately, per `21-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 6), `scroll-snap-stop: always` is documented as materially weaker under proximity and specifically unreliable on iOS WebKit, and even a successful snap arrives through a spring settle the threshold reads mid-flight. The result the developer reported is text that flashes on and straight back off.

There is a second, sharper reason this cannot be left alone after plan 21-11. Until now a slide was `100svh` — reliably SHORTER than the live `innerHeight` whenever Safari's toolbar was retracted — and `computeSlideVisibleRatio`'s `min(rect.height, viewportHeight)` denominator therefore let the ratio sit at exactly 1 across a whole BAND of scroll positions. Plan 21-11 sizes slides from the live viewport instead, which is the correct fix for gap 4 and collapses that band to nothing: after it lands, `ratio >= 0.98` is a window roughly 17px wide on an 852px screen. A demanding threshold got more demanding. Lowering the reveal side and adding a release side are both required, and the second is what actually closes the gap.

The gap's second `missing` item — "reconsider whether the slides need a firmer snap guarantee than proximity provides on iOS" — is answered here explicitly, and the answer is no: see assumption A9. It is reconsidered and declined with reasoning, not skipped.

- **A9 — The page's `scroll-snap-type: y proximity` is deliberately left unchanged.** Reasoning: the only firmer snap mode is `mandatory`, and the page itself is the scroll container (D-08 requires scrolling past the last slide into the real footer, so a nested scroller is out of the question — `scroll-snap-type` has no effect on a non-scrolling wrapper, which is why the shipped rule lives on `html:has(.home-scroll-deck)` in the first place). <mechanism_note>
## The latch, stated once

Let `r` be a slide's own `computeSlideVisibleRatio` for the current frame, `R` the reveal threshold and `E` the release threshold, with `E < R`.

**Today:** `revealed = r >= 0.98`, recomputed from scratch every frame. The state has no memory, so the show condition and the hide condition are the same line, and the boundary is infinitely thin.

**After this plan:** `revealed = wasRevealed ? r >= E : r >= R`. The state has exactly one frame of memory, and it is memory the code already reads — `wasRevealed` is captured from the live class list on the line above the toggle today, for the accent rising-edge guard. Nothing new is stored, nothing new is written, and the function stays pure.

**Why `R > 0.5` is load-bearing:** every deck slide is one viewport tall and they are contiguous, so two adjacent slides' visible ratios sum to at most 1. A reveal threshold above 0.5 therefore guarantees at most ONE slide can cross into the revealed state at any scroll position, which is the property D-09's live accent and the 21-08 next-slide warm both ride on (they fire on the rising edge of exactly one slide). Drop `R` to 0.5 or below and two galleries can be "arrived" at once, and the accent starts tracking whichever one the array order happened to visit last. Do not lower it.

**A consequence worth knowing before it surprises anyone:** after plan 21-12's pull-up the first slide's top edge reaches 85px within the last ~9% of the zoom scrub, so the first slide now latches slightly BEFORE zoom completion rather than exactly at it. This is invisible and harmless — plan 21-12 made the stage opaque and stacked above the slides for precisely that span, so the first slide's accent panel is covered until the stage retires — and its only real effect is that D-09's accent write and the 21-08 next-slide warm both fire a few dozen pixels earlier, which for the warm is a small improvement. Do not "fix" it.
</mechanism_note>
